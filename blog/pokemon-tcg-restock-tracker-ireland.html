How It Works

It's pretty simple. Our bot checks every store's website (and some in-store inventory systems) every 2 minutes. When a product goes from out-of-stock to in-stock, you get a Discord ping within seconds. The alert includes the product name, price, stock quantity, and a direct link to buy.

You choose which stores you care about, so you're not getting spammed with alerts from shops on the other side of the country. If you only care about your local Smyths in Blanchardstown and the Tesco in Liffey Valley, that's all you'll get pinged for.

FAQ

Is it actually free?

Yes. Smyths alerts for all 35 stores plus community channels are completely free, no time limit. Elite is optional if you want more stores.

How fast are the alerts?

We scan every 2 minutes. From the moment stock goes live to the Discord ping landing on your phone — usually under 30 seconds. For high-demand products, that speed is the difference between getting one and missing out.

Do I need Discord?

Yes, alerts are sent via Discord. If you don't have it, it's a free app — takes 2 minutes to set up. Make sure push notifications are enabled so you get pinged even when the app is closed.

Can I set alerts for specific stores?

Absolutely. You pick exactly which Smyths locations (and Tesco counties, Art N Hobby branches, etc.) you want alerts for. No spam from stores you'll never visit.
